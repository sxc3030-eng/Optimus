# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Using the project's tools for malicious purposes against unauthorized targets
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Responsible Use

Optimus is a **defensive** cybersecurity suite. Contributors and users must:

1. Only use offensive tools (RedTeam, StrikeBack) against systems you own or have written authorization to test
2. Never use Optimus tools for unauthorized access, DDoS, or any illegal activity
3. Report vulnerabilities responsibly (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))
4. Respect the privacy and security of others

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at **conduct@netguardpro.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
